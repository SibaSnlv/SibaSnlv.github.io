Fraud Detection · Operational Analytics PostgreSQL · Python · Rule-Based Detection

Payment Operations Analytics

"A mobile money payment system processed 6,362,620 transactions over 30 days. During that period, 8,213 transactions were fraudulent. The system's built-in fraud detection flagged 16 of them."

The Problem

With a 0.19% detection rate, the payment system missed 8,197 fraudulent transactions. The operational patterns of the fraud needed to be identified without waiting for a complex machine learning infrastructure build.

The Solution

A PostgreSQL analytical pipeline that derives operational flags in SQL. By analysing balance anomalies and intraday patterns, I designed three simple rules that catch approximately 81% of confirmed fraud.

Concentration

100% of fraud

Occurred exclusively in TRANSFER and CASH-OUT transaction types.

Timing

Midnight to 5am

Fraud rates spiked to ~70% when human monitoring was minimal.

Detection Recall

47.90% Caught

By a single rule tracking zero-balance wipeouts.

Data Analytics · Economics Power BI · Python · Stats SA QLFS 2008–2026

SA Labour Market Analytics Dashboard

"A six-page interactive Power BI dashboard built on real Stats SA QLFS data, combining a Python cleaning pipeline, DAX time intelligence, and linear regression projections to analyse South African unemployment from 2008 to 2026 Q1 across provinces, sectors, youth demographics, and a 2-year forward outlook."

The Problem

South Africa's unemployment crisis is widely discussed but rarely visualised with rigour. Existing public dashboards use static PDFs or aggregated national figures that obscure the structural inequality between provinces, sectors, and demographic groups.

The Solution

A full analytics pipeline, from raw government Excel files to a live interactive dashboard. Python parses and reshapes 657 province-quarter observations. Power BI models a star schema with DAX measures for QoQ and YoY change. Six pages cover national overview, provincial analysis, youth focus, sector trends, 8-quarter projections, and written insight summaries.

Key Finding

28.3% avg unemployment

masked by 16pp gap between best and worst province

Key Finding

40.3% youth rate

12pp above national avg — structural, not cyclical

Key Finding

Mining declining since 2012

Finance and community services only consistent growth sectors
